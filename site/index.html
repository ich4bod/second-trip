Second Trip

Treasure is only safe at the gate. A heavy pack burns twice the fuel on every road.

Start a different expedition

Changing the starting lantern begins a new game. Start again keeps this choice. A flask can still refill up to twenty fuel.

The low lintel refuses a pack heavier than three. Go through the Well, or lighten the pack. Either arch still falls after the first banked haul.

The way back to the gate

This takes one ordinary road. It does not bank treasure, leave the dungeon or complete the whole return.

This route keeps your current pack and takes no new items. A carried flask refills only after banking at the gate.

On the floor

Taking something and getting home

These previews take one item, keep the resulting pack and collect nothing else on the way home. A carried flask refills only at the gate.

Set down one carried item and take this floor item in one choice. No fuel is spent. Undo reverses both parts.

In your pack

Put all carried treasure on this floor in one choice. Keep the flask, if you have it. Nothing is safe at the gate until you bank it.

Dropping costs no fuel, but the item stays in this room. The return estimate keeps everything else in your pack.

At the gate

Bank one carried item to keep the rest in your pack. The first banked item still closes the short arch. Banking only the flask spends it for fuel.

No attempt kept.

Return restores this whole attempt and its starting lantern. It clears the current Undo steps; it does not spend a road or bank treasure.

Taking, dropping and banking cost no fuel. Roads do. You can carry at most six weight; above three, every road costs double. Fuel does not run on a clock. Banking your first haul closes the Hall–Vault arch in both directions. The other roads stay open. Undo can take you back before it fell.
